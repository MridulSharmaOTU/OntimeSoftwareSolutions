Description

Final Fantasy VII: Rebirth continues the journey of Cloud and his companions as they leave Midgar and venture across the world. This action RPG delves deeper into the battle against Shinra and the enigmatic Sephiroth, blending real-time combat with strategic elements. Players explore a vast overworld filled with new challenges, secrets, and character-driven narratives.
