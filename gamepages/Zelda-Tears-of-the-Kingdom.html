Description

Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is an action-adventure game that builds upon the open-world gameplay of its predecessor, Breath of the Wild. Players guide Link through the skies, underground depths, and the lands of Hyrule to uncover secrets, solve puzzles, and prevent Ganondorf from destroying the kingdom. The game introduces new abilities and environments, expanding the scope of exploration and storytelling.
